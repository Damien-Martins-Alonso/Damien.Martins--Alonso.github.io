📌 Featured Engineering Project

Miniature Equatorial Mount Design (TIPE)

2025 - 2026

Engineered an equatorial mount to compensate for Earth's rotation. Analyzed dynamic modeling and managed machining constraints (overconstrained system h=4). Developed the embedded control using Arduino, integrating light sensors with anti-reflective shielding and a camera module for fine tracking.

Result: Reduced light trailing by 5x and achieved a theoretical sharpness gain of 42x.
