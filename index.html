🔭 Project

Miniature Equatorial Mount (TIPE)

2025 - 2026

A miniature equatorial mount project carried out at the end of preparatory classes : the goal is to compensate for the Earth's rotation in order to capture high-resolution deep-sky astrophotography. The orientation and dynamic modeling are controlled by an Arduino board, integrating light sensors with anti-reflective shielding, while a custom Python image processing algorithm handles the fine tracking via a camera module.
